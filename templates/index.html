Medicine Expiry Alert SystemProject overview & objectives

Project overview

The Medicine Expiry Alert System tracks medicine batch and expiry information and notifies users before medicines expire. It supports individuals, pharmacies, hospitals, and medical stores in monitoring medicine stock and availability, reducing waste and helping prevent expired medicines from being used or sold.

Each record can include medicine name, batch number, manufacturing date, expiry date, quantity, and calculated stock availability. Expiry dates are checked automatically whenever the dashboard refreshes.

System objectives

  • Monitor medicine expiry dates automatically.
  • Alert users 90, 30, and 7 days before expiry.
  • Reduce medicine waste and financial losses.
  • Maintain accurate medicine and stock records.
  • Clearly identify expired medicine to help prevent use or sale.
  • Support medicine stock availability management.
